## 1. Agro-Climatic Profile of North Eastern Horticultural Produce

The North Eastern Region (NER) of India produces premium horticultural commodities characterized by high moisture content, rapid post-harvest metabolic respiration, and susceptibility to both high-temperature decay and chilling injury.

This manual provides validated, literature-grounded storage setpoints programmed directly into the **SCAB Crop Database**.

## 3. Crop-Specific Storage Details & Best Practices

### A. Green Chilli & Bhut Jolokia (Capsicum chinense)
- **Economic Value**: Bhut Jolokia (GI Tagged) commands up to ₹400–800/kg in external markets. Rapid decay and desiccation in transport cause massive financial losses.
- **Vulnerability**: Highly susceptible to **chilling injury** below 7°C (manifested as surface pitting, calyx browning, and skin shriveling).
- **Controlled Atmosphere Protocol**:
  - Oxygen: $3.0\text{--}5.0\%$
  - Carbon Dioxide: $< 4.0\%$
  - Effect: Prevents chlorophyll degradation, retaining deep green coloration and pungency (Capsaicinoid content) for 4 weeks.

### B. Cabbage (Brassica oleracea var. capitata)
- **Production**: Grown in massive volumes in Meghalaya and Assam valleys during winter.
- **Optimal Conditions**: High relative humidity (95–98%) is critical to prevent outer leaf moisture loss and trimming wastage.
- **Controlled Atmosphere Protocol**:
  - Moderate $CO_2$ (up to 5%) suppresses fungal pathogen sporulation (*Botrytis cinerea*).
  - Low temperature ($0\text{--}2^\circ\text{C}$) supported by PCM ice buffering extends shelf life up to 50 days.

### C. French Beans (Phaseolus vulgaris)
- **Characteristics**: High metabolic respiration rate ($q_{\text{resp}} \approx 120\,\text{mW/kg}$ at harvest).
- **Vulnerability**: Pods suffer from russeting and water-soaking if held below 5°C.
- **SCAB Protocol**: Earth-Air pre-cooling strips field heat immediately after harvest. Controlled atmosphere ($O_2 \approx 3\%$) stops pod toughening (fiber development) and moisture loss.

### D. Leafy Mustard Greens (Lai Xaak)
- **Significance**: Core staple green vegetable in Assamese and North Eastern indigenous cuisine.
- **Challenge**: Enormous surface-area-to-volume ratio causes wilting within 24 hours under ambient conditions.
- **SCAB Benefit**: High humidity mist retention and $2\%\,O_2$ storage maintains turgor and vitamin C content for up to 18–20 days, enabling regional export.

## 4. Operational Guidelines for Farmer Cooperatives

1. **Harvest Timing**: Harvest produce during early morning hours (05:30 to 08:30 AM) when internal produce temperature is lowest.
2. **Sorting & Grading**: Discard mechanically damaged or disease-infected vegetables before loading to prevent ethylene spikes.
3. **Pre-Cooling Verification**: Check the SCAB app for the green **"Cold Priming Complete"** banner before opening the chamber door.
4. **Loading Limit**: Do not exceed the rated 300 kg chamber loading limit per cycle to maintain adequate air circulation channels.
5. **Door Discipline**: Minimize door openings to under 45 seconds to prevent air exchange and retain the conditioned $N_2$ atmosphere.
